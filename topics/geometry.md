## Let the diagram organize the information

In geometry, mark the facts you know before calculating: equal sides, a right angle, parallel lines, or a shared arc. Then choose a rule that uses those facts. For a triangle, the angle sum is 180°; for a right triangle, side lengths follow Pythagoras; for similar shapes, side and area ratios scale differently.

### Walk through: an isosceles triangle

If the vertex angle is $40^\circ$, the two equal base angles share the remaining $180^\circ-40^\circ=140^\circ$. Each is $140^\circ/2=70^\circ$.

**Sanity check:** $40+70+70=180^\circ$, and the equal sides face equal angles. **Your turn:** a triangle has angles $50^\circ$ and $60^\circ$. Find the third. *(Answer: $70^\circ$; subtract the known angles from 180°.)*

## Exam shortcut playbook

| If you spot... | Shortcut |
|---|---|
| Triangle angles | Use $180^\circ$ total before doing anything else. |
| Isosceles triangle | Equal sides have equal opposite angles. |
| Right triangle with familiar sides | Check $3:4:5$, $5:12:13$, or $8:15:17$ before using a square root. |
| Similar triangles | Match corresponding sides first; square the ratio only for areas. |
| Regular polygon | Each exterior angle is $360^\circ/n$; interior $=180^\circ-$ exterior. |
| Circle angles on the same arc | The central angle is twice the inscribed angle. |

## Ten worked examples

### 1. Angles in a triangle
**Question:** A triangle's angles are $2x,3x,4x$. Find the largest.
- **Step by step:** Their sum is $180^\circ$: $9x=180$, so $x=20$.
- **Fast way:** Largest is 4 of 9 equal parts: $4(20)=80^\circ$. **Answer: $80^\circ$.**

### 2. Exterior angle
**Question:** Two opposite interior angles are $45^\circ$ and $65^\circ$. Find the exterior angle.
- **Step by step:** The third interior angle is $70^\circ$; its adjacent exterior angle is $110^\circ$.
- **Fast way:** Add the two opposite angles: $45+65=110^\circ$. **Answer: $110^\circ$.**

### 3. Find a right-triangle side
**Question:** A right triangle has legs 9 cm and 12 cm. Find the hypotenuse.
- **Step by step:** $c=\sqrt{9^2+12^2}=\sqrt{225}=15$ cm.
- **Fast way:** Recognize the $3:4:5$ triple scaled by 3. **Answer: $15$ cm.**

### 4. Compare similar-triangle areas
**Question:** Corresponding sides are in ratio $2:5$. Find the area ratio.
- **Step by step:** Area scales as side ratio squared: $2^2:5^2$.
- **Fast way:** Square both side parts. **Answer: $4:25$.**

### 5. Find a regular-polygon angle
**Question:** Find each interior angle of a regular hexagon.
- **Step by step:** Angle sum $=(6-2)180=720^\circ$; divide by 6.
- **Fast way:** $(n-2)180/n=4(180)/6$. **Answer: $120^\circ$.**

### 6. Find the third triangle angle
**Question:** Two angles of a triangle are $38^\circ$ and $77^\circ$. Find the third.
- **Step by step:** Subtract the known angles from $180^\circ$.
- **Calculation:** $180^\circ-38^\circ-77^\circ=65^\circ$. **Answer: $65^\circ$.**

### 7. Find a missing leg
**Question:** A right triangle has hypotenuse 13 cm and one leg 5 cm. Find the other leg.
- **Step by step:** Use $a^2+b^2=c^2$ and subtract the known leg's square.
- **Calculation:** $\sqrt{13^2-5^2}=\sqrt{144}$. **Answer: $12$ cm.**

### 8. Use the isosceles shortcut
**Question:** An isosceles triangle has a vertex angle of $40^\circ$. Find each base angle.
- **Shortcut:** The two base angles are equal; together they are $180^\circ-40^\circ=140^\circ$.
- **Answer:** Each is **$70^\circ$**.

### 9. Find a circle's central angle
**Question:** An inscribed angle is $37^\circ$. Find the central angle on the same arc.
- **Shortcut:** Double the inscribed angle.
- **Calculation:** $2(37^\circ)$. **Answer: $74^\circ$.**

### 10. Find a regular polygon's exterior angle
**Question:** Find each exterior angle of a regular 10-sided polygon.
- **Shortcut:** Exterior angles always total $360^\circ$.
- **Calculation:** $360^\circ/10$. **Answer: $36^\circ$.**

## Quick practice

1. A right triangle has legs 6 and 8. Find the hypotenuse. *(Answer: 10; $\sqrt{6^2+8^2}$.)*
2. Find each interior angle of a regular pentagon. *(Answer: $108^\circ$; $(5-2)180^\circ/5$.)*
3. Similar figures have corresponding side ratio $3:4$. What is their area ratio? *(Answer: $9:16$; square the side ratio.)*
