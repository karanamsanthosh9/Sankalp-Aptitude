# Simplification & Approximation

High-yield notes for SSC, RRB, APPSC, and PSU quantitative aptitude.

## 1. Core Concept & Fundamental Equations

- Follow **BODMAS**: brackets, orders (powers/roots), division and multiplication left to right, addition and subtraction left to right.
- Convert terminating decimals to fractions when helpful: $0.125=1/8$, $0.25=1/4$, $0.2=1/5$, $0.75=3/4$.
- Laws of exponents: $a^m a^n=a^{m+n}$; $a^m/a^n=a^{m-n}$; $(a^m)^n=a^{mn}$; $a^0=1$ for $a\ne0$.
- For approximation, round only after identifying the precision required. Keep track of whether the question asks for an exact value or a nearest option.
- Useful squares: $11^2$–$20^2=121,144,169,196,225,256,289,324,361,400$.

## 2. Universal Shortcut Engine

| Technique | Use it when | Avoid it when |
|---|---|---|
| Cancel before multiplying | Fractions contain common factors. | Terms are being added; cancellation does not distribute over addition. |
| Fraction conversion | Decimals match familiar fractions. | Repeating decimals or many unrelated denominators make fractions slower. |
| Near-base arithmetic | Values cluster around $10,100,$ or $1000$. | Rounding errors could change close answer choices. |
| Option estimation | Options are widely separated. | Exact equality or close options demand exact arithmetic. |
| Exponent laws | Bases or exponents can be combined. | Addition/subtraction is mistaken for multiplication. |

## 3. Model-Wise Question Classification

### Model 1: Order of operations
**Problem:** Evaluate $48\div6\times(3+5)-7$.
- **Method A:** Brackets first: $3+5=8$; then left to right, $48/6\times8-7=64-7=57$.
- **Method B:** Reduce the bracketed multiplier: $48/6=8$, then $8\times8-7=57$. **Answer: $57$.**

### Model 2: Fraction cancellation
**Problem:** Evaluate $\frac{18}{35}\times\frac{14}{27}$.
- **Method A:** Multiply then simplify: $252/945=4/15$.
- **Method B:** Cross-cancel: $18/27=2/3$ and $14/35=2/5$, giving $4/15$. **Answer: $4/15$.**

### Model 3: Decimal-to-fraction conversion
**Problem:** Evaluate $0.125\times640$.
- **Method A:** Multiply directly: $640\times0.125=80$.
- **Method B:** Replace $0.125$ with $1/8$: $640/8=80$. **Answer: $80$.**

### Model 4: Approximation with nearby bases
**Problem:** Estimate $49.8\times20.2$.
- **Method A:** Expand $(50-0.2)(20+0.2)=1000+10-4-0.04=1005.96$.
- **Method B:** Round to $50\times20=1000$; for a close estimate, include the small correction, approximately $1006$. **Answer: about $1006$.**

### Model 5: Exponent laws
**Problem:** Simplify $2^5\times2^3/2^6$.
- **Method A:** Evaluate: $32\times8/64=4$.
- **Method B:** Combine exponents: $2^{5+3-6}=2^2=4$. **Answer: $4$.**

## 4. Exam-Specific Alignment & Variations

- **SSC lens:** Nested brackets, fractions, recurring decimals, surds, and options designed to punish careless left-to-right evaluation.
- **RRB lens:** Fast arithmetic, decimal operations, squares, and approximation under time pressure.
- **AP State / PSU lens:** Multi-step expressions and arithmetic blended with fractions, indices, and roots.

## 5. Common Traps & Speed Killers

1. **Doing multiplication before division by habit:** multiplication and division have equal precedence; work left to right.
2. **Canceling across addition:** simplify factors only, never terms separated by $+$ or $-$.
3. **Over-rounding:** keep enough significant digits to separate close options; calculate exactly when choices are close.
