# Mixtures & Alligation

> **ALLIGATION:** cross-subtract from the target; opposite difference gives the quantity.

## ⚡ Formula cards

| Problem | Formula |
|---|---|
| Average price / strength | **$\frac{\text{total value}}{\text{total quantity}}$** |
| Alligation: low : high | **$(H-M):(M-L)$** |
| Amount of solute | **$\text{concentration}\times\text{total quantity}$** |
| Repeated replacement | **$V(1-\frac{x}{V})^n$** |

**Alligation cross-card**

```text
 Low value L       High value H
       \             /
        \           /
         Target M
      /             \
 H - M               M - L
  low amount         high amount
```

> **Memory cue:** Cross-subtract from the target; each difference belongs to the *opposite* ingredient.

## Exam shortcut playbook

| If you spot... | Shortcut |
|---|---|
| Two prices/strengths and a target | Cross-subtract around the target; low:high $=(H-M):(M-L)$. |
| Given quantities of ingredients | Use weighted total divided by total quantity. |
| Water added to a solution | Solute stays fixed; solve for the new total volume. |
| Part of a mixture removed and replaced | Original ingredient remaining $=V(1-x/V)^n$. |
| A target outside the two ingredient values | Check the question: mixing cannot produce a value outside their range. |

## Ten worked examples

### 1. Find the mixing ratio
**Question:** Rice at $40$/kg and $60$/kg is mixed to make $48$/kg. Find the ratio.
- **Step by step:** Let quantities be $x,y$. $40x+60y=48(x+y)$, so $12y=8x$.
- **Fast way:** Low:high $=(60-48):(48-40)=12:8$. **Answer: $3:2$.**

### 2. Find the mixture's average price
**Question:** Mix 3 kg at $40$/kg with 2 kg at $55$/kg. Find price per kg.
- **Step by step:** Total cost $=120+110=230$; divide by 5 kg.
- **Fast way:** Weighted average $=40+(2/5)(55-40)$. **Answer: $46$/kg.**

### 3. Add water to milk
**Question:** A 30 L mixture is 80% milk. How much water makes it 60% milk?
- **Step by step:** Milk is $30(0.8)=24$ L. At 60%, total volume must be $24/0.6=40$ L.
- **Fast way:** Milk stays fixed; final total is 40 L, so add 10 L. **Answer: $10$ L.**

### 4. Replace part of a mixture twice
**Question:** A 20 L vessel is full of milk. Remove 5 L and refill with water twice. How much original milk remains?
- **Step by step:** Each round keeps $15/20=3/4$ of the milk. After two rounds: $20(3/4)^2=11.25$ L.
- **Fast way:** Remaining milk $=V(1-x/V)^n$. **Answer: $11.25$ L.**

### 5. Blend two solution strengths
**Question:** Make 30 L of 40% solution using 20% and 50% solutions.
- **Step by step:** Let $x$ be litres of 50% solution: $0.5x+0.2(30-x)=12$, so $x=20$.
- **Fast way:** Low:high $=(50-40):(40-20)=1:2$. **Answer: 10 L at 20%, 20 L at 50%.**

### 6. Find the amount of pure solute
**Question:** How much salt is in 25 kg of a 12% salt solution by mass?
- **Step by step:** Convert the percentage to a decimal and multiply by the solution mass.
- **Calculation:** $0.12(25)=3$. **Answer: $3$ kg of salt.**

### 7. Replace part of a mixture once
**Question:** A 40 L container is full of juice. Remove 10 L and refill with water. How much juice remains?
- **Step by step:** Each replacement leaves $1-\frac{10}{40}=\frac{3}{4}$ of the original juice.
- **Calculation:** $40(\frac{3}{4})=30$. **Answer: $30$ L of juice.**

### 8. Use alligation for prices
**Question:** In what ratio should rice at $50$/kg and $80$/kg be mixed to get a blend at $60$/kg?
- **Shortcut:** Low:high $=(80-60):(60-50)=20:10$.
- **Answer:** Mix **2 parts of the $50 rice to 1 part of the $80 rice**.

### 9. Add water without changing solute
**Question:** How much water must be added to 40 L of a 25% solution to make it 20%?
- **Shortcut:** Solute stays at $40(0.25)=10$ L-equivalent. Final volume $=10/0.20=50$ L.
- **Answer:** Add **10 L of water**.

### 10. Replace the same amount twice
**Question:** From a 30 L full container, remove 10 L and refill twice. How much original liquid remains?
- **Shortcut:** Each round keeps $1-10/30=2/3$ of the original liquid.
- **Calculation:** $30(2/3)^2$. **Answer: $13\frac13$ L.**
